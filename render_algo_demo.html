💡 Algorithm Theory Explanation
Loop Invariants & Time Complexity

Bubble Sort repeatedly steps through the list, comparing adjacent pairs and swapping them if out of order:

C(n) = n(n-1)/2 ∈ O(n²) | Space: O(1) Auxiliary
  • Loop Invariant: After pass $k$, the largest $k$ elements are guaranteed to be in their final positions at the right (shown in 🟩 Emerald Green).
  • Active Swap (🟥 Crimson): Elements $38$ and $12$ are adjacent out of order ($38 > 12$). A positional swap is actively executed in $O(1)$.
  • Worst-Case Scenario ($O(n^2)$): Reverse sorted array requires $\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$ comparisons and equal swaps.
  • Stability: Maintains relative order of equal items because it only swaps when strictly greater ($A[j] > A[j+1]$).
