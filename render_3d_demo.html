💡 Astrophysical Explanation
Keplerian Orbital Dynamics & Gravity

Planetary bodies follow elliptical orbits bound by the Sun's central gravitational pull, described by Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation:

F = G · (M_sun · m_planet) / r²
  • Kepler's 3rd Law: Orbital period squared is proportional to the semi-major axis cubed ($T² \propto a³$). Closer planets orbit significantly faster.
  • Mercury (Inner): Moves at 47.9 km/s with an 88-day orbit.
  • Saturn (Outer): Moves at only 9.7 km/s, taking 29 Earth years.
  • Atmospheric Scattering: Earth's blue halo is caused by Rayleigh scattering, deflecting short blue wavelengths through the atmosphere.
