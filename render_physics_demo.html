💡 Physics Law Explanation
Momentum Transfer & Torque Cascade

When the projectile hits the first domino, kinetic energy is converted into rotational angular momentum:

p = m · v | τ = r × F | e = (v₂' - v₁') / (v₁ - v₂)
  • Impulse Collision ($J = \Delta p$): The steel cannonball transfers sudden linear momentum into the lead domino.
  • Torque & Center of Mass: Impact force applied at height $h$ generates an unbalancing torque $\tau = h \cdot F_{\text{impact}}$, tilting the center of mass past its base.
  • Cascade Propagation: Each falling domino converts gravitational potential energy ($U = mgh$) into rotational kinetic energy, accelerating the cascade.
  • Restitution ($e = 0.45$): An inelastic collision factor determines how much energy is dissipated as vibration upon contact.
